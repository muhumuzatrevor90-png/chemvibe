ABOUT CHEMVIBE

Making chemistry
accessible to everyone.

An independent student study hub built to help university chemistry learners grasp tough concepts, access organized study materials, and learn together.

OUR MISSION

Why ChemVibe exists

University chemistry can feel overwhelming when notes, past papers, and explanations are scattered. ChemVibe brings concise topic guides, practice materials, and live study sessions into one clean, distraction-free space.

WHAT WE OFFER

Built for real studying

Students can filter through curated notes and past papers in the library, read bite-sized concept breakdowns on the blog, and join live interactive chemistry sessions from anywhere.

INDEPENDENT PROJECT

Student-first focus

ChemVibe is an independent educational initiative focused on clarity, practical problem-solving, and helping fellow students build confidence in physical, organic, and inorganic chemistry.

THE CREATOR

Meet Trevor Mbabazi

ChemVibe was created and developed by Trevor Mbabazi with a simple goal: to make studying chemistry clearer, better organized, and less stressful for university students. Combining a passion for chemistry education and modern web development, Trevor built ChemVibe from the ground up as a dedicated space where learners can find reliable study notes, practice problems, and live support.
