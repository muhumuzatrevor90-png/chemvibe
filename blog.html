FOUNDATIONS

Atomic structure

Atoms contain a nucleus of protons and neutrons, with electrons occupying regions around it. Proton number identifies the element; neutron number can vary between isotopes.

INORGANIC

Periodic trends

Across a period, effective nuclear charge generally increases. Down a group, additional occupied shells affect atomic size and shielding. Use these ideas to explain trends rather than memorizing arrows alone.

PHYSICAL

The mole and stoichiometry

Balance the equation, convert known quantities to moles, use the coefficients as mole ratios, then convert to the requested units. Check your units and identify limiting reactants when needed.

ORGANIC

Functional groups

Functional groups are recurring atom arrangements that influence a molecule’s properties and reactions. Learn to recognize groups such as hydroxyl, carbonyl, and carboxyl.

BONDING

Ionic and covalent bonding

Ionic models describe attraction between charged ions; covalent models describe shared electron pairs. Many bonds have mixed character, so treat these as useful models.

INORGANIC

Redox basics

Oxidation is electron loss and reduction is electron gain. Oxidation-state changes provide a bookkeeping method for identifying what is oxidized and reduced.
