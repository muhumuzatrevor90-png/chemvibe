Guidelines & Navigation

  • 👤 Username & Identity: Updating your username changes how your name appears in your welcome banner and private messages with your instructor.
  • 🖼️ Profile Picture: Click Change Picture to upload a square photo so your instructor can easily recognize your account.
  • 🔒 Private Q&A Messaging: Questions sent on any Chemistry Library resource are 100% private—only you and the instructor can view or reply to your thread.
  • 🔑 Password Security: Use at least 6 characters. If you haven't signed in recently, sign out and log back in before changing your password.
  • 📱 Mobile Navigation: Tap the top-right three-dash menu icon (☰) on your phone to switch between Library, Blog, Live Classes, Notifications, and Profile.
